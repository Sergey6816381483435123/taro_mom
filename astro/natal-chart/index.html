Natal chart and interpretation

A natal chart represents celestial positions at the time of birth. In Astro, you can explore the chart and its interpretation as an introduction to astrology and a prompt for personal reflection.

The diagram, portrait and full reading

The chart is calculated from birth details and represents positions of celestial bodies. Its astrological interpretation connects elements with symbolic themes about personality and experience. It is not a scientific assessment of character.

The diagram, short portrait and full AI interpretation are distinct results. The diagram presents the chart, the portrait introduces its interpretation, and the full reading offers a more detailed account.

Prepare your details and begin

Enter your birth date, time and place when the app asks for them. Time and place affect the calculation, so uncertain details can change the chart. Check a reliable record if one is available.

Open the natal chart section and complete the requested fields. For example, explore the diagram and short portrait before reviewing the conditions for a full interpretation. Personal information is entered in the product, not on this website.

Reading with perspective

Choose a theme that interests you and consider it alongside your own experience. An interpretation does not establish that someone necessarily has a particular trait.

Access to the diagram, portrait and full reading may differ. The app shows costs, limits and any Premium requirement before you confirm an action.

Questions, answered

FAQ

Can I use an uncertain birth time?

The app asks for the details needed for a personal calculation. An uncertain time can lead to a different chart and interpretation.

Is the short portrait the full reading?

No. The portrait offers a brief introduction; the full AI interpretation explores the themes in greater detail.

Does the chart prove what someone is like?

No. Natal charts are part of astrology and are intended for entertainment and self-reflection.
