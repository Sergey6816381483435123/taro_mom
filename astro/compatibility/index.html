How synastry works

A natal chart is calculated for each person, then the two charts are compared. The interpretation considers their combination within astrology. It does not measure compatibility scientifically or establish how a relationship will unfold.

For example, you might use the reading to consider what expectations you bring to a relationship or what you would like to discuss. The answers belong to you and the other person.

What to prepare

You will need birth dates, times and places for both people. Ask the other person for their details and make sure they agree to share them. Accurate information matters because it is used to calculate the charts.

Open compatibility in Astro and complete the requested fields. If the action is paid or requires Premium, the app shows the conditions before confirmation.

Synastry, love horoscopes and Tarot

Synastry compares two natal charts. A love horoscope is a different, shorter astrology format. A relationship Tarot reading uses cards and the context of your question.

Choose the approach that interests you. None of these formats establishes another person’s feelings or replaces an open conversation.

Questions, answered

FAQ

Can I use only our zodiac signs?

For synastry in Astro, you need both people’s birth dates, times and places.

Is it only for romantic partners?

A comparison can prompt reflection on interactions between two people. It does not establish the quality of a relationship.

Can it tell me how someone feels?

No. An astrological interpretation cannot establish another person’s feelings or intentions.
