Moon calendar and daily journal

The Moon calendar in Astro provides information about the Moon, including its phase. An optional daily journal lets you record mood, energy and a note about your day.

Explore the Moon calendar

A lunar phase describes the visible illuminated portion of the Moon. The calendar brings current Moon information together in one part of the app.

If you enjoy an astrological perspective, use it as a topic for observation and reflection. A Moon phase does not by itself establish an effect on health, mood or the success of an activity.

Keep an optional journal

Open the journal beside the Moon section and, if you wish, record your mood and energy or add a short note. For example, write about something that helped you rest today or something you want to do tomorrow.

There is no obligation to make an entry. The history lets you return to earlier notes. Add only the information you would like to retain in the product.

Do you need birth details?

The Moon calendar does not require personal birth information. Natal charts, transits and other personal calculations ask separately for the details they need.

Open the Moon section and explore the available information. Current conditions for individual actions are shown inside the product. This website does not collect personal journal entries.

Questions, answered

FAQ

Do I have to write every day?

No. The journal is optional; use it when you want to record something.

Do I need my birth time and place?

Personal birth details are not required for the Moon calendar.

Can a Moon phase tell me whether a plan will succeed?

No. The calendar offers Moon information, not a reliable prediction of your actions’ outcome.
