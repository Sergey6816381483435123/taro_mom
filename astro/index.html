Find a format for your question

A natal chart represents celestial positions at birth. For relationships, compare two charts in compatibility or choose a love horoscope.

Transits and personal astrological events relate celestial positions to your natal chart. A solar return offers a symbolic view of a personal year. The Moon calendar provides Moon information alongside an optional daily journal.

What information will you need?

Personal calculations ask for the relevant birth date, time and place. Compatibility needs details for both people; a solar return also needs a year and calculation location. The Moon calendar does not require personal birth details.

You enter the details inside the product, not on this website. When exploring another person’s chart, ask them for the information and make sure they agree to share it.

Accessing Astro

Both Astro and Tarot are available in the Mini App and browser app. In a browser, sign in through Telegram or with a verified email address and password. Shared profile data and credits require the same account or explicitly linked identities.

Some features are free and some actions are paid. Conditions depend on the feature; the app shows any price and Premium requirement before confirmation.

Questions, answered

FAQ

Are Astro and Tarot the same thing?

They are different areas. Tarot uses cards for symbolic readings; Astro offers astrology formats. Both are available in the Mini App and browser app.

Do I need my birth time?

Personal calculations request the necessary birth details, including time. The Moon calendar does not require personal birth information.

Can astrology reliably predict the future?

No. Astro content is intended for entertainment and self-reflection, not reliable prediction.
