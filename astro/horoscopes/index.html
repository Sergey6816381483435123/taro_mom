Daily and love horoscopes

Astro offers a daily horoscope, a personal love horoscope and a love horoscope for two. Each is a short astrology format for reflecting on a day or on relationships.

Choose a horoscope

The daily horoscope focuses on the day and is a place to start with a brief astrology reading. The personal love horoscope focuses on relationship themes from your perspective.

The horoscope for two considers a pair. To compare two natal charts, explore the separate compatibility section: it is a different format with fuller birth details.

Get a result and return to it

Open Astro and choose a horoscope. The app asks for the information needed for that format; a reading involving another person may also require their birth details. The product indicates the period covered.

When you return to a previous text, access may depend on the format and saved result. The app shows the price and requirements for a new action before confirmation.

Keep the reading in perspective

Treat a horoscope as a symbolic prompt, not an account of events that must happen. For example, consider an idea about a conversation or rest alongside what you know about your circumstances.

A horoscope cannot know another person’s plans or guarantee how your day will unfold. Base practical decisions on reliable information and your actual situation.

How is a personal love horoscope different from one for two?

The personal format focuses on your relationship themes; the other considers two people.

Can I read a previous horoscope again?

Conditions depend on the format and saved result. Check its availability inside the app.

Does it predict the day?

No. It is intended for entertainment and self-reflection, not a reliable prediction of events.
