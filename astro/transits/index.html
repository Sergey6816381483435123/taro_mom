Transits and your natal chart

A natal chart represents celestial positions at birth, calculated from your birth date, time and place. A transit reading considers positions on another date in relation to that chart.

The natal chart provides the starting point; the transit reading adds a selected date and an astrological interpretation. It is not a scientific forecast of life events.

Use it as an invitation to reflect

An interpretation might suggest a theme about attention or revisiting plans. Ask yourself whether you have practical reasons to make a change and what small step would be useful.
