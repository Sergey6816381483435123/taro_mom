What is a solar return?

A solar return chart is calculated for the moment the Sun returns to its position at birth. In astrology, it is used to consider themes of a personal year.

The interpretation can offer a prompt to think about priorities and plans. It does not establish which events will happen during that period.

Information to prepare

You need your birth date, time and place, along with a year and location for the solar return calculation. Check the information before submitting it: these details are used to build the chart.

Open solar return in Astro, complete the fields and review access conditions. The app shows any credit or Premium requirement and price before confirmation.

How it differs from other formats

A natal chart refers to the moment of birth; a solar return offers a separate astrological view of a personal year. A year-ahead Tarot reading uses the symbolism of cards and their positions.

For example, after reading a solar return interpretation, write down your own priorities and consider which real resources you need for them. Make decisions using your circumstances and reliable information.
