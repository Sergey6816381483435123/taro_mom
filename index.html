FAQ

What is Taroshenka?

Taroshenka is an AI-powered Tarot reading bot in Telegram. You choose a spread, ask a question and receive card images with an interpretation in the chat.

Does it predict the future?

No. Readings are for entertainment and self-reflection: they offer a symbolic perspective, not a guaranteed prediction.

Which readings are available?

The bot offers Daily Card, Yes or No, Three-card, Dawn, a five-card relationship reading and four Premium readings.

How does Premium work?

Premium includes Choice Between Two Options with 7 cards, Work & Finances with 7, Celtic Cross with 10 and Year Ahead with 12 cards.

How are payments handled?

Packages of 5, 10 or 30 credits and monthly Premium are available. The bot shows current terms before purchase.

Which languages are supported?

The bot supports 20 languages listed above, with Arabic and Persian interfaces using right-to-left direction.

A note about Tarot

Tarot readings and AI interpretations are intended for entertainment and self-reflection. They are not a reliable prediction of the future and do not replace medical, psychological, legal, financial or other professional help. You make important decisions yourself.
