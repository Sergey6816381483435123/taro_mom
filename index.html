From a question to a connected interpretation

Choose a format that fits your question. When a reading asks for context, describe the situation in your own words: what is happening, what feels uncertain and what you would like to explore.

View the drawn cards and read an AI interpretation that connects their positions, combinations and upright or reversed orientation. Your question provides context; the conclusions and decisions remain yours.

Start small or explore in more depth

Begin with a Daily Card, Card Advice, Yes or No, or a three-card reading. Relationship formats include Our Relationship, New Love, What Do They Feel? and Will They Return? Their names describe the topic of a symbolic reading, not reliable knowledge of someone else’s feelings.

  • Explore a situation with Near Future, My Next Step or How the Situation Develops.
  • For a broader perspective, compare two options or choose Work & Finances, the Celtic Cross or a year-ahead reading.
  • Some extended formats require Premium. Current access conditions are shown inside the product.

Return to a reading and explore it further

Suitable results can be followed by a clarifying question or an additional card in the context of the original reading. History lets you revisit saved questions and interpretations.

In the app, open a card image and, when the setting is enabled, read its reference meaning. The reference explains the symbolism; the AI interpretation relates it to your reading. Your preferred language and deck are saved in your profile.

Free features, credits and Premium

The product includes free features, such as the Daily Card in Telegram. Paid actions use credits. Premium opens extended Tarot formats and some Astro features; it does not mean unlimited use.

The bot or app shows the price and requirements before you confirm an action. Access can depend on the format, subscription, trial eligibility or a saved result. Check current purchase terms inside the product. The Wheel of Fortune is a separate feature and does not guarantee a credit reward.

Questions, answered

FAQ

What is Tarot?

Tarot is a Tarot service with AI interpretations, available in a Telegram chat, the Mini App and a browser. The app also has a separate Astro area.

Which entry point should I choose?

Open Tarot for readings in a chat. To use the visual app inside Telegram, open Astro and launch it with its Telegram button. Choose the browser entry to use the app outside Telegram.

Can I use it without Telegram?

The browser app supports Telegram sign-in and registration with a verified email address and password. A separate email account is linked to Telegram only after confirmation.

What does Premium include?

Premium unlocks a range of extended features. Some actions still use credits; the product shows the applicable requirements before confirmation.

Does it predict the future?

No. Readings offer a symbolic perspective for entertainment and self-reflection, not a reliable prediction or a substitute for professional help.
