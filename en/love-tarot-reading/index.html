Reflect on the dynamics of a relationship

Our Relationship is a five-card reading about the dynamics between people. It offers a symbolic view of expectations, experiences and possible directions, while leaving conclusions and decisions to you.

Describe what matters to you

Explain the relationship and the question on your mind. For example: “We talk less often now, and I want to think about how to discuss it.” Context helps connect the interpretation to your subject.

You do not need to share unnecessary personal information about the other person. Feelings and intentions are matters for real communication, not established facts from a reading.

What the five cards offer

Each card has a position, and the AI interpretation connects their meanings into one reading. Position labels and card images are available with the result.

The reading offers several aspects to consider. It does not prove that someone feels a certain way or establish how a relationship will unfold.

Other relationship questions

The catalogue also includes New Love, What Do They Feel? and Will They Return? These names describe subjects for symbolic readings. Some formats require Premium; the product shows the conditions before confirmation.

For a different approach, Astro offers compatibility through a comparison of two natal charts. It is a separate format that uses both people’s birth details.

Questions, answered

FAQ

Can I find out someone’s actual feelings?

No. A reading cannot establish another person’s thoughts or feelings. You can use it to formulate your own questions and prepare for a conversation.

Does it guarantee that a partner will return?

No format guarantees another person’s actions or the outcome of a relationship.

How is it different from synastry?

Tarot uses cards and the context of a question. Synastry compares two natal charts and needs birth details for both people.
