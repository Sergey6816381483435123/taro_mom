Explore a situation through three connected cards

A three-card reading is a compact way to explore a question through both individual meanings and their connections. Taroshenka builds one AI interpretation from your question and the cards’ positions.

Why the connections matter

Each card has a role in the spread. The interpretation considers the cards together to create a connected perspective. The actual positions and their labels are shown in the result.

Not every three-card format uses past, present and future. Read the positions of the selected format and consider how they relate to your question.

Give the question a little context

For example: “I want to return to a project I put aside. What should I consider?” Add relevant context without combining several unrelated situations into one short reading.

View the cards in order, then read the overall interpretation. It can help you formulate your own next step; it does not choose that step for you.

After the reading

Return to a saved result in your account history. Where supported, a clarification or an additional card lets you explore the original context further. The product shows the price and requirements before confirmation.

Questions, answered

FAQ

What do the three positions mean?

They depend on the selected format and are shown in the result. Do not assume that they always represent past, present and future.

Can I return to the answer?

Saved readings are available in your account history. To share access across entry points, use the same account or identities that have been explicitly linked.

Do I need Premium?

The bot or app shows the selected format’s current requirements. Premium unlocks some extended formats and does not mean unlimited access.
