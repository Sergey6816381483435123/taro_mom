A daily Tarot card for reflection

The Daily Card is a simple introduction to Tarot through one card and its short meaning. Get it free in the Telegram bot and choose a theme to reflect on during your day.

One card, one question for yourself

Look at the image and read its meaning. You do not have to treat it as a literal forecast. Instead, notice whether a theme connects with your current thoughts or plans.

For example, you might ask yourself what deserves your attention today. Use the answer as a note to yourself or a starting point for a more detailed reading.

How to get your Daily Card

Open Tarot in Telegram and start the chat to receive a Daily Card with its image and a short meaning.

The Daily Card and Wheel of Fortune are different features. The Wheel involves a spin and a possible reward; it does not replace the card reading or guarantee a credit.

When you want more context

For a specific situation, explore Card Advice, Yes or No, or a three-card reading. The bot shows the requirements before a paid action. The app also offers card viewing and optional reference meanings.

Questions, answered

FAQ

Is the Daily Card free?

Yes. The current Telegram catalogue offers the Daily Card free of charge. Other actions have their own conditions, shown before confirmation.

Does it predict what will happen today?

No. It offers a symbolic theme for reflection, not a reliable account of future events.

How is it different from Card Advice?

The Daily Card is a general daily prompt. Card Advice is a separate short reading for a personal question, with its own access conditions.
