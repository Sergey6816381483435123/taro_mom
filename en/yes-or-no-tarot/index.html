A focused yes-or-no Tarot reading

Yes or No is a one-card reading for a focused question. Taroshenka explains the card in context, giving you something to consider rather than making the decision for you.

Ask one clear question

Choose one situation and be specific about what you want to explore. “Should I discuss my new responsibilities with my manager?” provides clearer context than several unrelated questions at once.

The format offers a symbolic direction. For an important decision, consider the interpretation alongside reliable facts, your circumstances and professional advice when needed.

What the reading includes

One card is drawn. The AI interpretation relates its symbolism and orientation to your question, offering an explanation beyond a single yes or no.

View the image and notice which ideas are useful to you. The card cannot establish another person’s intentions or guarantee the outcome of an action.

How to begin

Open Taroshenka or the app, choose Yes or No, enter your question and review the action’s conditions. After confirmation, explore the card and interpretation. For a question with several connected aspects, consider a three-card reading.

Questions, answered

FAQ

Can I ask several questions at once?

For this short format, choose one focused question. It makes the context easier to follow.

Is the answer guaranteed to be right?

No. It is a symbolic interpretation for self-reflection, not a reliable prediction or verified fact about a situation.

What works better for a complex question?

A three-card reading or another relevant format can help you consider several connected aspects.
